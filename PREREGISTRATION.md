## 1. What is registered, and its status (R0.1)

A creature is made three times by hand on one real page: drawn in ink, painted in gold and cut from paper. Phone takes of the page are regenerated by a video model so that one creature comes to life, steps up out of its place on the page and answers something real beside it: a glass ramekin of water, a torch's light, a pen, a breath, a handwritten note or a hand. Each creature answers by the laws of its own material. The laws are sealed (§18).

Registered here, before any generation:
- what is rendered and how (§4, §14–§16);
- how each render is aligned to its take and measured, and what counts as "kept" and "redrawn" (§5–§12);
- one hypothesis, H, with its test and verdict rule (§13);
- the cold rating study, its questions and what it reports (§17);
- the kill rules, the budget and the order of events (§19–§21).

**Success criteria** (the card's, registered here):
1. A stranger can wipe any take and see what was kept, what was redrawn and which cue fired which law.
2. Three to five cold raters (two in person, the rest remote; call 115) rate the eight renders, with the hit rate per law reported against a still baseline, with intervals, null or not.
3. The film (60–120 s, ending on the drawing still on the page) and the thesis are published and linked from the portfolio.

Also: this registration is public before any generation, and H is reported whatever it shows.

**Status.** Exploratory. H covers the eight rated renders only. Three of its seven testable renders share one take (the glass take, with each creature acting), so a supported H could rest on few takes; the write-up says so. The curtain call (§4) is never rated and never measured for H.

The beam lands on the home with the largest mean lit share over the run's first 8 frames, if that share is at least 0.05. The note detector (call 132) reads inside the note track's box in its last tracked frame, padded by 10 px; read on the whole frame, OCR took the ink creature's legs for letters.

## 5. Analysis grid and time

- Everything is measured on a 1280×720 grid at 15 analysis frames per second, on the render after it has been warped onto the take frame by frame.
- Each stream keeps its own presentation timestamps. Analysis time i/15 takes the first decoded frame whose timestamp is at or after i/15 less half a source frame (`seam.masks.sample_frames`); for constant-frame-rate video that is the frame nearest i/15, a tie going to the earlier frame. The grid runs from 0 to the last timestamp. Frames are resized onto the grid with area interpolation. A stream more than half a source frame short of the span is refused.
- Every render is gated as it downloads, before any study is built.

## 6. Alignment gate

**Time.** Motion energy e(t) is the mean absolute grey difference (0–255) between consecutive frames inside the take's hand and prop masks at t. The render's energy is measured inside the same take masks, on the render's own frames before warping. The best lag maximises Pearson r over lags −5 to +5 analysis frames; a tie goes to the smaller |lag|, then to the negative one. A positive lag means the render is late. The check passes if |lag| ≤ 1.
- *Active frames* (call 126): the frames where the take's hand or prop masks are non-empty. The take's mean motion energy is taken over these frames only, so a hand in shot for 2 s of an 11 s take is not averaged away.
- *Not applicable:* a take with no active frame (the breath take, which has no hand and no prop), or one whose mean energy over its active frames is below the floor of 1.0 grey level per frame (as drafted, kept on the chosen takes: t01f 2.17, t02e 6.23 and t03c 1.45 are applicable; the note and the hand lie still in t05c, 0.41, and t06c, 0.81, and t04d has no active frame). The reason is recorded, and alignment rests on the 90 % rule below.

**Space.** For each frame, a similarity transform from render to take:
- ORB, 4,000 features, one pyramid level, on both greys after a Gaussian blur of sigma 1 px, with keypoints refined by `cv2.cornerSubPix` (11×11 window, 30 iterations, eps 0.01);
- features only outside the take's page mask dilated by 64 px, the same mask on both frames (so the render's creature mask isn't needed);
- Hamming brute-force matching with cross-check;
- `cv2.estimateAffinePartial2D` with RANSAC: 3 px, 2,000 iterations, confidence 0.999, 10 refine iterations, OpenCV's RNG seeded 20261031 before each fit;
- fewer than 20 matches or 20 inliers: no fit, and the frame scores None;
- residual = the RMS reprojection error of the inliers.

A frame passes if its residual is ≤ ρ and its scale is within 1 ± 0.01, where ρ = max(1.0 px, 2 × the 99th percentile of the residuals of the take fitted against its own 720p round trip). If every round-trip fit fails, ρ is undefined and the render fails with that reason. A frame that fails scores None.

**A render is aligned** if at least 90 % of its frames pass, it covers the take's analysis span, and the lag check passes or is not applicable.

**Trimmed inputs** (call 135). A render made from a trimmed input (the probe's and the no-op's first 10 s, a draft's 4 s window) is gated against the same span of the take, its frames and masks: render frame j against take frame first + j, where the span is the request's recorded trim (start and seconds, rounded to analysis frames; `align.py gate --start --seconds`). Its duration is checked against that span, and the gate record carries it. The gate records its parameters and the opencv-python, numpy and av versions.

**Warp.** Each passing frame is warped onto the take grid with its transform: bilinear for images (edge pixels replicated), nearest-neighbour for masks. A take-grid pixel is *covered* if the inverse transform of its centre lands inside the render frame ([0, w − 1] × [0, h − 1]); the rest form the border strip that the warp fills by replicating the render's edge. Uncovered pixels leave R(t) (§7; call 126).

**Registered ρ per take:** 1.0 px (the floor) on every take, t01–t06, with no failed round-trip fit (192, 199, 192, 189, 194 and 205 fits), from `runs/registration/<take>.rho.json`.

## 7. Regions and masks

**Masks** are SAM 2.1 small tracks at the pinned commit, on CPU, propagated forward from each object's first prompt (frames before it are empty). Mask logits > 0. No hand edits.

- **Take masks** cover the page, the three creature homes, the props, the hand and the note. The blind agent (below) places point prompts on grid-overlay frames from a frozen brief (`agents/briefs/take-mask-points.md`), with each object named by an opaque letter and a plain description. Aaron checks a contact sheet; a rejection, by a reason from a fixed list, allows one fresh placement. The prompts (`masks/prompts/<take>.json`), the ledgers and the mask digests are in the manifest.
- **Render masks** come from one positive point on the acting creature at its **prompt frame: the first sampled still in which no part of the creature overlaps its home** (the spot where it was drawn; call 108). Frames before it count as the creature at home. A pick can land up to stride − 1 frames after the true departure; those frames count as at home, which adds creature lines to R away from contact, so the error works against H.
  - *Who picks it:* a blind agent (`blind-frame-picker`), not the controller, who knows the laws. It sees every 3rd analysis frame of the render as numbered stills (5 a second; every 4th, 5th or 6th if that is the first whose folder fits the 16 MB a session can take, decided from the video before any attempt and recorded in the ledger: `frame_pick.choose_stride`, call 135; every 3rd fits all seven takes, at 12.5–13.5 MB), with the acting creature's home drawn on each as a thin grey dashed outline from the take's home mask, and a one-line rule (`agents/briefs/prompt-frame.md`); no law, cue, take or material name. "none" means the creature never leaves its home: the render then has no render mask and is **not testable for H**, and is reported as such.
  - *The point:* the same agent, in a fresh call, sees only the prompt frame (plain and on a grid, the home outlined) and places one positive point (`agents/briefs/render-mask-points.md`). A second fresh call always checks the first track's outline at that frame; it accepts it or gives the one second point (positive or negative) that the re-prompt allows. A null point, like "none", leaves the render with no render mask. So does a pick or point step that closes as failed (both attempts used, or a call lost after dispatch): the render is then not testable for H, and is reported as such.
  - *How each call runs:* through `seam/dispatch.py`, as a bare `claude -p` session from the folder itself (no project context; Read only, confined to the folder; the model pinned). Every folder is first logged as a `prepared` attempt in its one ledger and in an append-only registry, so no answer can be chosen among several, and a rewound ledger shows. The dispatcher writes the session's event stream itself as the audit transcript, and Claude Code's session store is the cross-check. A call that reached the API but whose record cannot be trusted (a store missing, duplicated or not matching the stream) is *lost after dispatch*: the step closes as failed, with no fresh attempt, and is disclosed in A1. Only a call where nothing reached the API frees a fresh attempt. Nothing the agent writes reaches a committed file as text. Two attempts per step; a valid answer is final, and nobody edits it. The assembler and the lines job use a render's ledgers only when they are committed and unmodified at HEAD.
  - *The outline's accuracy:* the home is drawn on the render's own frames, unwarped, so it carries the alignment gate's residual error (at most ρ, a pixel or two, on frames that pass).
  - The masks are tracked on the render's analysis frames (any 16:9 source, sampled onto the 1280×720 grid as the agent saw it) and warped with the frame's transform. The ledgers, prompt files and digests go into addendum A1 before the lines job runs, and the lines job and the assembler refuse a render whose pick, points or track digest are not logged.
- **Mask quality rule.** From the frame after the prompt frame on, a frame scores None if the creature mask is empty, or if its area changes by more than 50 % from the previous frame. Exactly +50 % passes. A non-empty mask after an empty frame is an unbounded change and fails. The prompt frame passes by definition, and so does every frame before it: there the creature is its drawing at home, A(t) = dilate(home, m), and its mask is empty, so it makes no contact (`masks.quality_ok(start=prompt frame)`; built 3 Nov, `d9fa384`).

**A frame is scored** only if it passed the alignment gate and the mask quality rule.

**Measured region.** A(t) = dilate(creature_render(t) ∪ home, m) and R(t) = ~A(t) ∩ cover(t), where home is the acting creature's home mask, m is set by the paste control (§10): 32, 48 or 64 px, and cover(t) is the set of pixels the frame's warp covers (§6). Dilation is by a Euclidean disk. Uncovered pixels are never measured: they are not part of D(t), and their cells get no verdict (§11), so the border strip a warp leaves cannot read as redrawn or pull κ_render_p1 (§9) towards 0 (call 126). The codec null is scored under the render's own R(t).

**Contact.** c(t) = dilate(creature_render(t), 8 px) ∩ (touched object ∪ hand) ≠ ∅, on the take's masks.

## 8. Masked line drift D(t)

StillDrawn's frozen `_line_maps` (LineartAnimeDetector, `detect_resolution` 1024, per-map Otsu) on both frames, then the A9.4 frame rule restricted to R(t), with a = the render's map and b = the take's map at the same t, and full-frame Euclidean distance transforms:
- None if a or b is empty;
- if a∩R is empty and b∩R is not: the one-sided term EDT(~a)[b∩R].mean() (lines that vanish from R count as drift);
- if b∩R is empty and a∩R is not: the mirror term EDT(~b)[a∩R].mean() (lines that appear in R count as drift);
- if both are empty in R: None;
- otherwise 0.5 · (EDT(~b)[a∩R].mean() + EDT(~a)[b∩R].mean()).

With R all-true this equals StillDrawn's `_chamfer` bit for bit, and it equals `metrics/masked.py` in StillDrawn's private working repo (`d4b8fde`) on every branch; both are pinned by tests. D(t) is None on any frame that is not scored (§7).

If the no-op render fails the alignment gate, κ_keep falls back to κ_codec. The no-op render gets the finals' technical re-render rule (§16). How much the model redraws when asked to change nothing (its D, and its kept and redrawn cell shares) is reported in its own right.

**κ_keep:** ⟨set after Gate P2 by the no-op render; not known at Gate R. Recorded with its source, `kappa_render_p1` or `kappa_codec`⟩.

## 10. Controls

Both run free on the real takes, before registration, through the lines job with the frozen extractor.

**Paste.** The acting creature's scan crop is alpha-composited onto the take (alpha > 127 is the paste mask), with the room untouched and no re-encode, so the control isolates creature lines leaking across the margin.
- *Placement:* the crop is held still through the cue window, placed so that its nearest mask pixel lies at least 4 px from the touched object's take mask at the cue frame (the first 0.25 px step along the line at which it does, rounded to whole pixels: 4.0–5.0 px in the registered placements), on the line from the touched object's centroid to the acting creature's home centroid. SAM tracks forward from the blind placer's prompt frame only, so the touched object's mask can be empty at the cue frame; the crop is then placed against its first tracked frame at or after the cue frame, and the window still starts at the cue (call 133: t05's note is tracked 3 frames after its cue, t06's hand 7). Both frames are recorded.
- *Cue window:* the analysis frames from the firing event's t (in the take's cue sheet) to the end of the take.
- For each m in 32, 48 and 64: R_m(t) = ~dilate(paste mask(t) ∪ home, m); D(t) = the frame rule, composite against take, in R_m; D0(t) = the frame rule, codec null against take, in R_m. The threshold is the linear 95th percentile of D0's non-None values over the window. A margin passes if at least 20 frames are scored and at least 95 % of the frames with D not None have D ≤ the threshold.
- The codec null is the one at the render model's own output resolution: 1080p for aleph2, which keeps its input resolution, and 720p for kling_4 and seedance2_5. (gemini_omni_flash_1.1 renders 640×360, but §14 never makes it the final model.) The final model is chosen after registration, so the control is run and registered at both.
- *One m for the study:* at each resolution, m is the smallest of 32, 48 and 64 that passes on all seven renders with a touched object. If none passes on all seven, H is registered as not testable at that resolution. The breath render is run and reported (crop placed just below its home, off the page) but does not vote.

**Registered:** m(720p) = 32, m(1080p) = 32. Every render, the breath render included, passes at every margin on every scored frame at both resolutions (pass fraction 1.000; 135–164 frames scored a render; at m = 32 the thresholds are 0.030–0.056 px at 720p and 0.066–0.083 px at 1080p). Per render id and margin, the threshold, the pass fraction and n scored are in `runs/registration/<render>.paste720.json` and `.paste1080.json`. Gate P2 picks the pair that matches the final model.

**Shift.** A 64×64 room patch (2×2 cells, at a cell-aligned origin) has its content moved by 3 px on each axis, (3, 3), so strokes in every direction are displaced.
- *Patch choice:* the 2×2 block fully inside R in every frame, with at least 20 line pixels per cell in every frame, with no dominant stroke orientation, and with the most line pixels; the first in row-major order on a tie. "No dominant orientation" means structure-tensor coherence below 0.5, from Sobel gradients of the line map smoothed by a Gaussian of sigma 1 px, weighted by the line pixels dilated by disk(2), summed over all frames.
- *Region before rendering:* R0(t) = ~dilate(home, m), on the take.
- A frame passes if all four of the patch's cells are classed redrawn (§11); a cell with no verdict counts as a failure. The control passes if at least 90 % of frames pass.
- It is run with κ_codec before registration, and rerun with the no-op κ_keep after Gate P2. Both are reported.
- A failure does not change H, which is computed on D, not on cells. A failure is reported, and the seam view carries a label saying what a 3 px move does to its room cells (call 133).
- *Room share* (descriptive, call 133): the whole take is moved by (3, 3), its line maps are extracted, and every room cell (§11) is classed against the take under R0 at the same κ. The strip the move leaves uncovered is not scored. Reported as the shares of room cells redrawn, no verdict and kept (`lines_job.py shift-room`).
- The shift pair is measured only through `lines_job.py shift-control`, never through `lines_job.py measure` (call 126).

**Registered:** the shift control **fails on every render at both resolutions** (`runs/registration/<render>.shift720.json` and `.shift1080.json`).
- *The patch:* (384, 96) on the stand takes (coherence 0.064–0.096) and (320, 288) on t02 (0.363). The pass fraction is 0.000 everywhere: no frame has all four cells redrawn.
- *Why:* the rule takes the densest terrazzo. There the dilated lines cover 62–82 % of each cell, and a 3 px move leaves IoU at 0.38–0.58, above κ/2 (0.431 at 720p, 0.377 at 1080p).
- *Room share* (`<render>.shiftroom720.json` and `.shiftroom1080.json`):

  | | Redrawn | No verdict | Kept |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 720p | 69.6–73.8 % | 26.2–30.3 % | at most 0.12 % |
  | 1080p | 50.8–53.0 % | 47.0–48.4 % | at most 1.46 % (handheld t02) |

- **The seam view's label:** "On the textured desk, a 3 px move shows as redrawn in about 70 % of room cells at 720p and about half at 1080p. Elsewhere, in the densest texture, it shows as no verdict, and almost never as kept."

## 11. Seam cells

- 32×32 px cells on the take grid: 40 columns × 23 rows, the bottom row 16 px tall.
- A cell with any pixel in A(t) (outside R(t) but covered by the warp) is *creature*.
- Otherwise, a cell with any pixel the warp did not cover (§6) has no verdict, and is never a room cell.
- Tolerant IoU at τ = 2 px (StillDrawn's `iou_tolerance_px`): both full line maps are dilated by disk(2), and intersection and union are counted inside the cell.
- A cell is *kept* if its IoU ≥ κ_keep, *redrawn* if its IoU < κ_keep / 2, and otherwise *no verdict*. It also has no verdict if both frames have fewer than 20 (undilated) line pixels in it. Every cell of a frame that is not scored has no verdict.
- Room cells, for κ, are the cells fully inside the region with at least 20 line pixels in either map.
- The seam file holds 2 bits per cell per analysis frame (0 none, 1 kept, 2 redrawn, 3 creature): cells in row-major order, four per byte (c0 | c1<<2 | c2<<4 | c3<<6), each frame padded to a whole byte, no header.

## 12. The room's invariants and stays drawn

**Hand keypoints** (hand take). MediaPipe Hand Landmarker (the model hashed in §3), image mode, one hand, 21 points in pixels. Drift per frame = the mean distance between the take's and the render's points / the diagonal of the take hand's point box. None when either side has no hand or the frame is not scored (it failed the gate or the mask quality rule).

**The note's words** (note take). Apple Vision OCR at the accurate level, language correction off, on the note's box every 3rd analysis frame. Lines are sorted top to bottom, then left to right, and joined by a space. Character error rate = Levenshtein(take text, render text) / length of the take text, both upper-cased with whitespace collapsed; None when the take text is empty.

**Stays drawn** (descriptive, no verdict).
- The acting creature: line density inside its mask, and the chamfer to its scan's line map after moment alignment (centroid translation and isotropic scale by radius of gyration, no rotation, so a turned pose reads as drift).
- The two resting drawings: the frame rule inside each one's home mask, against the same for the codec null.

## 13. Hypothesis H: drift outside the creature peaks at contact (R0.4)

**Contact by design.** At Gate L each rated law was tagged with the object it touches and the moment it touches it. The tags are sealed. The touched object per take is the one in §4 (and in `capture/takes.json`); a sealed tag that disagrees with it blocks registration. The seven sealed tags name the §4 objects, as `tests/test_sealed_tags.py` checks: six word for word, and the torch take's tag names the standing object in other words, mapped by a sealed alias table (`sealed/tag-aliases.json`, §18). `seam/assemble.py` reads each render's touched object from `capture/takes.json` (`paste.touched_mask`), and the hand mask is the take masks' `hand` object for every take with a touched object (none for the breath take); neither is a command-line choice, and nor is the home or an extra record's name. `analysis/h_contact.py` accepts measure files only for the eight registered render ids, refusing any other and the curtain call by name; every expected testable render without a measure file is reported as not testable ("missing"), and t04-ink always as not testable (no touched object). The breath law touches nothing, so that render is not testable for H.

**Expected number of testable renders:** 7 (the rated laws with a touched object, confirmed from the sealed tags at Gate R).

**Eligible frames:** the touched object's take mask is present (non-empty) and D is finite (not None, NaN or ±inf).

**Per render:** r = mean D over eligible contact frames / mean D over eligible non-contact frames.

**Testable render:** aligned, with at least 8 eligible frames on each side, and a non-contact mean above 0 (otherwise r is undefined).

**Statistic:** T = the mean over testable renders of log r.

**Null:** each render's contact indicator is shifted circularly *within its eligible frames*, by k drawn uniformly from 0 to n − 1 (the identity included); 10,000 draws; one generator, `numpy.random.default_rng(20261101)`, drawing `integers(0, n_i, 10000)` per render in sorted render-id order. T and every T_π go through one code path, so a shift that reproduces the observed pattern gives a bitwise-equal value. One-sided p = (1 + #{T_π ≥ T}) / 10,001, and an undefined shifted r counts as ≥ T.

**Verdict:**
- **supported** if there are at least 4 testable renders, p ≤ 0.05 and the median r ≥ 1.25;
- **not testable** with fewer than 4 testable renders (no p is reported; T and the median r are);
- **not supported** otherwise.

**Secondary, no verdict.** The r variants print a p, each with its own `default_rng(20261101)`; the last three lines below are descriptive (the means over contact and non-contact frames, no p):
- r over all frames with D not None (the original reading);
- r within terciles of the take's motion energy in R, cut per render at the 1/3 and 2/3 quantiles over its eligible frames with a motion value (low ≤ the 1/3 quantile < middle ≤ the 2/3 quantile < high), with at least 3 frames a side;
- proximity: contact at d = 24 px;
- the touched object's interior also excluded from R (reflections and refraction live there): R(t) minus the touched object's take mask at t (`render_pair.py render --exclude-touched`, measured as the extra record `interior_excluded`);
- m sensitivity (the other two margins; extra records `m48`, `m64`) and d sensitivity (d = 4 and 16 px);
- ink only (six renders);
- time-matched r: mean D over contact frames / mean D over eligible non-contact frames within 2 s (30 analysis frames) of a contact frame, per render, with p by the same shift null within those frames (`h_contact.py`, call 136). Drift that only grows with time, with contact late, inflates r but not this;
- descriptive: the slope of D against t (per second) over eligible non-contact frames, per render, beside r;
- descriptive: the redrawn-cell share inside the touched object (cells at least half covered by its take mask) against the rest of R;
- descriptive: hand drift, contact against non-contact;
- descriptive: note character error rate, contact against non-contact.

Outputs are rounded to 12 significant digits and written as sorted JSON, so two runs give identical bytes.

## 22. Wording rules

- "Drift" means line structure moved, not that the model erred. Shadows, reflections and ripples count as drift.
- The hand's skeleton and the note's words are the invariants that should never change.
- Every number is labelled exploratory. H's verdict is stated in the registered words: supported, not supported or not testable.
- Hit rates are always printed with their baseline, interval and n.
- Line structure is a proxy for the room, not the room itself; the write-up says so.

## 23. Deviations policy

- After Gate R, any change to a registered rule, number, file, threshold or procedure is a deviation. It gets a dated entry in §24 before the step it affects: what changed, why, who decided (Aaron), what had been seen by then (renders, measures, ratings), and which results it touches. The manifest gains an addendum for any changed file.
- Wherever the registered analysis can still run, its result is reported beside the deviated one, first.
- A change to a law after Gate R is a deviation. Wording changes in a second draft round (§15) are allowed by the registration; they are logged, not counted as deviations.
- Technical re-renders under §16 are not deviations, but both renders are disclosed.
- A bug fix to registered code is a deviation: the entry gives the diff and says whether any output had been seen.
- Missing data are reported as missing, never imputed. A rater, render or frame is never dropped except by a rule registered here.
