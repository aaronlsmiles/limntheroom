# The human rating study: what raters are sent and shown

Frozen (version 1). Its SHA-256 is registered; it is never edited afterwards. This is the record of every word a human rater receives, in order. The wording on screen is generated by `rating/prepare.py` (its `TEXT`, `Q1_OPTIONS` and the sealed Q2 order); a test holds this file to that code and to the invite text in PREREGISTRATION §17, so the three cannot drift apart.

This committed copy holds a placeholder where the Q2 options go. The copy with the options filled in is sealed material (`sealed/briefs/study-human.md`), rendered at Gate R by `seam/blind.py render-brief`.

## 1. The invitation (sent by Aaron, one rater at a time)

Only the name, the link and the date are filled in. The link is the rater's own study id.

> Hi [name],
>
> Would you rate a short study for me? It takes about 15 minutes. You'll see eight still pictures, then eight short silent clips, and answer two multiple-choice questions about each. There's no answer I'm hoping for: I want your first honest read.
>
> Three requests:
> 1. Rate alone, in one sitting, with no one watching or helping.
> 2. Look nothing up. Don't search for anything about the pictures, the clips or the study.
> 3. Don't discuss it with anyone, me included, until I tell you the study has closed (in about a week).
>
> Before you start, please reply with this sentence: "I confirm that I have not seen any video, image, page, code or notes from the project this study belongs to, and I will rate alone and look nothing up."
>
> Your link is [link]. It's yours alone, so please don't forward it. The study closes on [date].
>
> Thank you!

## 2. Before the window closes, Aaron says nothing else about the study

If a rater asks anything about the study, the pictures, the clips or the project before the window closes, the only reply is:

> Thanks for asking. I can't say anything about it until the study closes, so that every rating stays a first read. Please answer with whatever seems right to you.

A rater whose declaration has not arrived before their ratings is not analysed. Each declaration and each reply, with its date, is kept with the rater's study id.

## 3. On screen

The study's title: A short rating study

**Part 1, the stills** (eight, one at a time, in an order seeded per session). The introduction:

> First, some **still drawings**, one at a time. Each has one drawing inside a ring.
>
> Two questions about each. Pick the one option that fits best.

For each still:
- If the ringed drawing came alive here, what would it react to?
- What would it do?

**Part 2, the clips** (eight, muted, looping, each beside its reference still: the same ringed still from part 1). The introduction:

> Each screen now shows a **still** (left) and a **clip** made from it (right, on a loop).
>
> Two questions about each clip. Pick the one option that fits best.

Under each clip: Watch the whole clip before answering.

For each clip:
- What did the creature react to?
- What did it do?

## 5. What raters never see before the window closes

No model, material or project name; no law, law label paired with a take, or prompt; no take name or render id (item codes are opaque); no tally; nothing indexed. The reveal shows codes only.
