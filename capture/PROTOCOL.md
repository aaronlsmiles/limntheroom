# Limn the Room: shooting guide (P47)

Seven takes, 10-12 s each, 2-3 attempts per take. About 21 clips. Hands only.
Read top to bottom once; then use the checklist at the bottom.

## Kit

- iPhone and a stand
- Torch (a bright, steady one)
- Glass of water (clear, plain, no logo)
- Uncapped pen
- The note: block capitals, one word, **JUMP**, on a small piece of plain paper
- The standing object for the torch take, standing on its long edge: a small, plain object (turn any logo away from the camera and watch for glints from its finish).
- The page with all three creatures (see Set). The making guide and the tracing templates: `creature/template/` (the manual is a private artifact).

## Phone settings

- 4K, 30 fps (call 120: every take at 4K 30 so the set matches; ingest scales them)
- HDR Video: off
- Action mode: off. Cinematic: off.
- Landscape
- No zoom (stay at 1x)
- Frame, then long-press the page until AE/AF LOCK shows. Check it stays locked between attempts.
- Stand: fixed height and angle for every take except the torch (handheld). Mark the stand's feet with tape so you can reset it.

## The set

- Clear desk. Nothing identifying: no screens, papers, letters, addresses, labels.
- The page is one A4 sheet, landscape, lying flat in the middle of the frame. Tape it down at the corners and never move it between takes.
- Three creatures on it, each 50 mm long, their centres on one line 70 mm from the far edge: ink at 55 mm from the left edge, gold at 148 mm (the middle), paper at 242 mm. That leaves about 43 mm between neighbours.
- The **near half** of the page (from about 100 mm from the far edge down to the near edge) is where props go.
- Camera: on the stand at the page's near edge, looking down at about 50-60 degrees, at 1x (never the 0.5x lens). Pull the stand back until the torch, the standing object and your wrist would sit wholly in frame (calls 127-128). Use one framing, the t01-glass-e framing, for every stand take (call 129); don't go wider, since the creatures are already about three grid cells long.
- Desk texture (calls 127-128): the printed terrazzo (`capture/texture/desk-texture-A4.pdf`), cut into strips and taped flat on the desk all round the page, without moving the page. The alignment needs it: the plain desk gave ORB nothing to fit.
- Hands only. No faces, no third party in shot.
- Look hard at the glass for reflections. Check each attempt for a face, a window, a screen.
- Even light, no flicker. Same lamp in every take. Close curtains if the daylight shifts. For the torch take, keep the room light as is.

## The seven takes

Rule for all takes (D11): props are set down and your hand has **left the frame before the cue moment**. The exception is the hand take. Hold still after withdrawing, then count out the rest of the 10-12 s without touching anything.

Stand-up order is the shooting order. Do all attempts of one take before moving on.

### t01 glass
- Purpose: the glass. One take, used for all three materials: ink, gold, paper.
- Creature: ink, gold and paper (left, centre, right).
- Handheld: no (stand).
- Blocking:
  - 0-2 s: empty page, hands out of shot.
  - 2 s: bring in the glass of water and set it down on the page, about 3 cm in front of the gold creature (nearer the camera). Let go.
  - 3 s: withdraw your hand out of frame.
  - 3-12 s: stay out. No hands, no movement.

### t02 torch
- Purpose: the torch.
- Creature: ink (left).
- Handheld: **yes**. Phone in one hand; this is the stress test, so do not brace it on anything.
- Setup: stand the standing object on its long edge in the gap between the ink and gold creatures, its long side running from the far edge of the page towards the camera. Keep about 1 cm clear of each creature.
- Blocking:
  - Before recording: the pen torch lies on the desk to the left of the page, level with the ink creature and pointing at it (towards the right), wholly in frame, tail switch included (call 127: an object cut off by the frame edge gets no mask). Long-press for AE/AF LOCK before pressing record, so the camera does not darken the room when the light comes on.
  - 0-2 s: phone in your right hand, framing the page. Torch off.
  - 2-3 s: switch the torch on at its tail (no pick-up). With the torch wholly in frame, a fingertip or part of the hand shows at the switch-on; that is expected and accepted (Aaron, 9 Nov). Take the hand straight back out of frame. The beam should land on the ink creature.
  - 3-12 s: keep the phone as still as you can. Do not follow anything.
- Note any glints from the object's finish.

### t03 pen
- Purpose: the pen.
- Creature: ink (left).
- Handheld: no (stand).
- Blocking:
  - 0-2 s: empty page.
  - 2 s: set the uncapped pen down on the page about 4 cm in front of the ink creature (nearer the camera), nib pointing at it. Keep it to the left of the gold creature's line. Let go.
  - 3 s: withdraw your hand out of frame.
  - 3-12 s: stay out.

### t04 breath
- Purpose: the breath.
- Creature: ink (left).
- Handheld: no (stand).
- Blocking:
  - No hand in shot at any point.
  - 0-3 s: empty page; you are out of shot, sitting still, to the side of the desk.
  - 3 s: one steady breath out from the left edge of the page towards the ink creature. About 2 s long. Sit to the left of the desk for this take.
  - 5-12 s: stay still and quiet.
- Keep your head and shoulders out of frame and out of the light.
- Phone setting for this take: Settings > Camera > Record Sound > Wind Noise Reduction **off** (it strips breath noise; call 123). Turn it back on afterwards if you like.
- Straw (Aaron, 7 Nov): tape a long or bendy drinking straw flat to the desk, left of the page, its tip pointing at the ink creature from the frame's left edge, ideally just outside the frame and never closer than 3 cm to the creature's tail. Set it before you press record, so it is there from the first frame and never moves. Blow into the far end from outside the frame. No hand touches it during the take; your head stays back and low, clear of the frame and of the light.

### t05 note
- Purpose: the note.
- Creature: ink (left).
- Handheld: no (stand).
- Blocking:
  - 0-2 s: empty page.
  - 2 s: set the note down flat on the page about 4 cm in front of the ink creature (nearer the camera), writing facing the camera and square to the frame. Keep it to the left of the gold creature's line. Let go.
  - 3 s: withdraw your hand out of frame.
  - 3-12 s: stay out.

### t06 hand
- Purpose: the hand. This is the one take where the hand stays.
- Creature: ink (left).
- Handheld: no (stand).
- Blocking:
  - 0-2 s: empty page.
  - 2 s: bring in your left hand from the left edge, palm up and flat, fingers together and relaxed, and lay it on the desk and page edge beside the ink creature, fingertips a thumb-width from its left side. Your arm must not cross the page. The wrist and a little forearm must be in frame (the hand detector needs the wrist; call 127): check in the viewfinder before recording. A small part of the wrist tattoo may show; that is accepted (Aaron, 9 Nov).
  - 3-12 s: hold perfectly still. Breathe quietly. Do not curl the fingers or follow anything.

### t07 curtain call
- Purpose: the curtain call. Not rated.
- Creature: all three (ink left, gold centre, paper right).
- Handheld: no (stand).
- Blocking:
  - 0-2 s: empty page. The torch is off, in your hand, outside the frame.
  - 2 s: switch the torch on from outside the frame and sweep its beam once across the page from left to right at an even, unhurried pace, taking about 3 s.
  - 5 s: torch off and out of frame, or set down off the page. Hand withdrawn.
  - 5-12 s: stay out of shot, nothing moves.
  - **The last 3-4 s must be completely still:** no hands, no light changes, no movement in frame. The clip is 10-12 s total, so start the stillness by 8 s at the latest.

## Per-take checklist

Tick before moving on, every attempt:

- [ ] Lock still on (AE/AF LOCK showing), no zoom
- [ ] 4K / 30 fps, HDR Video off, Action and Cinematic off
- [ ] Page unmoved; creatures all in frame and spaced apart
- [ ] Nothing identifying in shot; no face in any reflection
- [ ] Hand left frame before the cue moment (except t06)
- [ ] Light even, no flicker
- [ ] 10-12 s long; no early cut
- [ ] Named correctly (below)
- [ ] Shot note written in `sealed/shot-notes.md` if anything odd happened (kept private)

## File names

`tNN-name-x.mov`, where NN is the take number, name is as below, and x is the attempt letter (a, b, c).

- `t01-glass-a.mov`, `t01-glass-b.mov`
- `t02-torch-a.mov`
- `t03-pen-a.mov`
- `t04-breath-a.mov`
- `t05-note-a.mov`
- `t06-hand-a.mov`
- `t07-curtain-a.mov`

Do not delete bad attempts on the phone. Keep them all; we pick later.

## After the shoot

1. AirDrop the originals (not edited, not trimmed, not cropped, not filtered) to this repo's `raw/`. That folder is gitignored.
2. Never edit, trim, rename inside or re-export the originals. If you must change a name, use the pattern above and nothing else.
3. Tell Claude when they have landed; Claude hashes them (step 1.5).

## The flat scan of the page

For the creature crops. A still photo, not a video.

- Top-down, phone parallel to the desk, directly above the page.
- The whole sheet in frame, edges visible, with a small margin.
- Even light, no shadows: no hand, phone or torch shadow across the page; no glare.
- Page flat (tape the corners); sharp focus (tap to focus).
- Same page, same creature positions as in the takes. Do the scan once the page is final and before any take.
